System headroom

How much memory, CPU, and thermal margin the finder has. The verdict uses the worst reading since start-up settles (start-up is excluded until the solver and web UI are running and have settled), so a squeeze during a solve or slew shows up but the normal boot spike doesn't. Low RAM, swap growth and a busy CPU 0 must be sustained (about 15 s) to count; a one-off dip or burst doesn't. Swap here is compressed RAM (zram), judged on its real compressed size. Times are since the finder started.

{# ---- Hot-pixel / dark frame ---- #}

Dark frame (hot-pixel mask)

Builds a map of stuck/hot sensor pixels so they aren't mistaken for stars. Cap the lens (or close the cover) first, then capture. Captured at a long exposure and high gain (0.9 s, gain 16) so the mask covers every setting the finder uses; your live exposure/gain is restored automatically afterward.
