Dark frame (hot-pixel mask)

Builds a map of stuck/hot sensor pixels so they aren't mistaken for stars. Cap the lens (or close the cover) first, then capture. Captured at a long exposure and high gain (0.9 s, gain 16) so the mask covers every setting the finder uses; your live exposure/gain is restored automatically afterward.
