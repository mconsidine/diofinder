Reconstructed by the live solver (the same module that owns detection); temporal_median renders the solver's actual cached median stack.

        

Notes

  • row_percentile: cheapest; 25th-percentile of cache-line samples per row. Good for clean dark-sky frames with no gradient.
  • line_median: true per-row median; more robust to sensor row-noise and vertical brightness gradients.
  • column_percentile: subtracts per-column median before detection; removes vertical gradients (lens falloff top-to-bottom, sky-glow). Row variation still handled by scan-band RowPercentile. Per-frame only (cache fallback).
  • row_column_percentile: row-then-column separable background removal; covers most real gradients at ~2× line_median cost without morphological overhead. Per-frame only.
  • block_percentile: bilinear interpolation of per-tile medians; handles non-separable 2-D gradients (vignetting, nebulosity, light pollution) in a fraction of top_hat’s time. Tune the block size so it safely exceeds your largest star radius (default 32 for bin=2). Per-frame only.
  • top_hat: morphological white top-hat (image − opening); slowest but most thorough 2-D gradient removal. Requires sycamore ≥ 0.9.0 — older wheels silently degrade to line_median. Try block_percentile first; only switch to top_hat if block_percentile misses structure smaller than the block size.
  • The temporal cache (bg_cache_enabled) is orthogonal and stays active for row_percentile, line_median, and top_hat; the three spatial modes fall back to per-frame when the cache is steady.
