Notes

  • row_percentile: cheapest; 25th-percentile of cache-line samples per row. Good for clean dark-sky frames.
  • line_median: true per-row median; more robust to sensor row-noise, vignetting, and light-pollution gradients.
  • top_hat: morphological white top-hat (image − opening); removes 2-D gradients that per-row modes can’t see. Requires sycamore ≥ 0.9.0 — older wheels silently degrade to line_median. Tune the radius so it exceeds your largest star PSF (typically 12–16 px for the HQ Camera + 25 mm lens at bin=2). Run efinder-bg-test --inject-gradient 40 on the Pi to verify that star counts rise under a synthetic gradient.
  • The temporal cache (bg_cache_enabled) is orthogonal and stays active regardless of mode; it stacks frames over time for noise reduction and hot-pixel rejection.
